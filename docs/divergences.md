# Known divergences between `tyrian-sim` (Rust) and the C version

Reference: `docs/parity.md`. Across the 73 strict-parity golden masters
(370,005 ticks: `golden/*.gm`, `golden/campaign/*.gm`, `golden/endless/*.gm`
and `golden/detail/*.gm` — the latter at effects levels 1 and 3, excluding
`*.play.gm`), plus 5 replay golden masters (`*.play.gm`) and 5 recorded
replays (`*.rep`), **no group diverges**: player, scroll, enemies, player
shots, enemy shots, random generators and level end are identical at every
tick, with no tolerance in the comparator (`tyrian-golden --strict`). These
references are also rewritten byte for byte by the rewrite
(`tyrian-write-references --check`, ADR 0127). So there is no *measured*
divergence to date.

This document lists the divergences *known from reading the code*: C paths
that are not ported, or ported with an approximation, which the current golden
masters do not exercise. Each one states the group that would diverge, the
original C function and the reason for the choice. The "first tick" is "—"
as long as no golden master reaches it.

C bugs (out-of-bounds reads, undefined behavior): **eliminated**, never
reproduced (reframing of 2026-10-04). When a golden master goes through one of them,
the bug is fixed in the C too and the references are regenerated: the comparator
accepts no difference (strict parity). Entries concerned: 9, 10, 14.

Argument evaluation order (`f(rng_next() % a, rng_next() % b)`, unspecified in C): the reference GCC x86-64 binary evaluates right to left; reproduced and **proven** by counter-test for `varz.c:JE_doSpecialShot` (`e1-special-blade-autopilot`), the Nort sparks (`e1-nort-pulse`: reversed order → divergence at tick 108) and the random explosions (`e4-brainiac-autopilot`, `randomExplosions`: reversed order → `rng` divergence at tick 12,531).

Numbering: 27, 28 and 59 were each given to two entries; they are now 27a/27b, 28a/28b
and 59a/59b rather than renumbered, because other places cite these numbers (entry 54 cites
27b; the comment "divergence 59" in `rewrite/crates/tyrian-godot/src/playfield.rs` means 59a,
the shadows).

## C floating-point calculations reproduced in integers

ADR 0003 forbids floating point in the simulation. Three C calculations in
`float` influence the state; they are reproduced **bit for bit** by
`tyrian-sim/src/fmath.rs` (exact single-precision IEEE-754 rounding on
rationals), and are therefore not divergences:

- `enemy_render.c:JE_drawEnemy` — aimed shots and launches:
  `roundf((float)aimX / maxMagAim * aim)`;
- `mainint.c:JE_mainGamePlayerFunctions` — parallax:
  `floorf((float)(260 - (tempX - 36)) / (260 - 36) * (24 * 3) - 1)`;
- `enemy_spawn.c:JE_makeEnemy` — armor according to difficulty
  (`armor * 1.2f`, `armor * 1.8f`); the other factors (0.5, 0.75, 1.125,
  1.5, 2.5) are exact in binary and become integer divisions.

`varz.c:JE_doSP` (superpixels) calls `rng_next_float` three times per
particle; only the generator consumptions are reproduced (the
particles are rendering).


A test (`fmath::tests::matches_hardware_float_on_samples`) compares these
functions to the hardware `f32` on ~33,000 cases.

## Modern explosions: shockwave, flash, streak and debris (the maintainer's decision)

Intentional addition (the maintainer, 2026-10-11, proposal P3 "Measured spectacle", `artifacts/explosions-p3-final/`):
Setup > Graphics > Explosions = Modern keeps the C's Modern explosions as they are (`particles.c`: bright
fireballs, sparks, embers, debris, smoke puff and ring, directed spray, shockwave, boss flash and
slow motion) and adds on top of them, drawn over (`crates/tyrian-godot/src/modern_explosions.rs`), starting from the
impact point of the fatal shot and sized on the footprint of the destroyed sprites (number of pieces, value of
the enemy, boss event): a shockwave, a 2-frame white flash, a white horizontal streak, metal
shards and secondary explosions.

- **Small** (up to two 2×2 sprites, side by side or not, or a 24×28 footprint): standard explosion and 2
  sparks; no ring, no flash.
- **Medium**: white flash (radius 7 to 9 px), one ring of radius half the longest side (24 px on 48×28, 28 px
  on 48×56, cap 28), 3 px thick at birth then 1 px.
- **Large**: flash (12 to 20 px) and horizontal streak, three cascading rings, heavy shards including smoking ones.
- **Boss**: a chain of 5 to 8 small explosions spread over the hull during one second (each with a 5 to 7 px
  flash and a small hot ring of 14 to 20 px), then the final burst: flash, 280 px streak crossing the screen,
  three thin rings of 1 to 2 px that extend past the screen, golden shards and a large explosion at the center.

The ring is thick at birth (3 px medium, 4 to 7 px large) and thins to 1 px during a fast expansion (cubic, 4 to 7
frames), then fades; its final radius is, for a large one, 0.7 × the longest side of the footprint (40 to 110 px). Raiden II reference measured in video pixels (480 px wide) and scaled to Tyrian by 264 / 480 =
0.55 (terrain width / game width in the video); the radius follows the footprint and not this factor alone,
Tyrian's sprites being larger on screen than those of Raiden II. Same scale in Original and HD art.
Rings are hot (round 3, the maintainer, 2026-10-17, `artifacts/explosions-round3/`): white-yellow at birth,
orange halfway, dark red at the end, with a halo of 2 to 3 px around the stroke. The hollow red ring that ends
the game's fire explosion sprites (`newsh6.shp`, last frames of the explosion types 1 to 5 and 9, for example
frames 129 to 133 of type 1) is not drawn in Modern style: 5 embers of 1 to 2 px leave the explosion at that
moment, yellow-orange cooling to dark red, light gravity, 1 px trail, 10 to 18 frames; in Original art they take
the nearest colors of the level palette. The fire ball part of the sprites is unchanged.
No ring, flash or streak is drawn on an enemy shot (reservation). Rendering only: separate generators (kills,
boss chain, embers), identical simulation (test `modern_explosions_leave_the_simulation_untouched`, golden
masters unchanged).
Classic unchanged; the default style of a new profile remains Retro+.

**Less debris after a large explosion (the maintainer, 2026-10-20, playtest of v1.0-rc1: "a bit too much
debris after the explosions of medium-sized enemies").** `particles.c:particles_spawn_explosion_fx` (Retro+ and
Modern, explosion of `varz.c:JE_setupExplosionLarge`, i.e. the death of every 2×2 enemy piece) spawns 10 hot
embers and 4 grey chunks in the C; the rewrite spawns 5 and 2 (`fx_render.rs:LARGE_EXPLOSION_EMBERS`,
`LARGE_EXPLOSION_CHUNKS`), which also halves their trails, impact dust and landed embers. The 3 sparks, the smoke
puff, the four corner explosions, the kill spray and the boss burst (`on_boss_defeated`: 15 embers, 6 chunks) are
unchanged; bigger enemies keep their repeating large explosions. Rendering only (own generator), identical
simulation. Test: `fx_render::debris_tests`.

## DESTRUCT (artillery mini-game)

The mini-game is ported in `tyrian-sim/src/destruct.rs` (ADR 0132). The floating-point
operations (ballistics, gravity) are reproduced bit for bit by `f32ops.rs` (same technique
as for the level's floats). The frame-by-frame comparison against the C binary
(`rewrite/scripts/destruct_parity.sh`) gives **0 differing pixels over 368 comparable rows**.

The first 28 frames on the C side and the first 29 on the Godot side are fade phases
(palette initialization, `fade_black` + `fade_palette(25)`) that do not need to be compared
pixel to pixel: their rendering depends on palette rounding, not on the game logic.

## Title: exit confirmation with the box's true colors (the maintainer's decision, 2026-10-11)

Intentional difference. Upstream OpenTyrian2000 (commit aad5aca) quits immediately on Escape at the title: its confirmation box
(`game_menu.c:JE_quitRequest`) only exists in game and in the shop. The Enhanced C
added it to the title (`title_screen.c` → `JE_quitRequestMsg`), and the rewrite took it over
(`title.gd`): Escape, right click and the Quit entry ask for confirmation. But the box was
drawn there with the game palette's indices, displayed in the title palette (palette 8,

the maintainer decided to keep the confirmation, with no compatibility mode or setting, and to draw it
as in the shop. `TyrianVga.place_true_colour_box` (`quit_box.rs`) places the box's pixels

"No" restores the previous screen and palette, "Yes" keeps them for the fade. Where the palette is
already the game's (shop, save), nothing changes: the box is drawn directly.
Tests: `quit_box_colours_test.gd`, `cargo test -p tyrian-godot quit_box`.
