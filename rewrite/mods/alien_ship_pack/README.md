# Alien Ship Pack

Eight ships flown by the two alien races of Tyrian's story: the **Hazudra**
(Trent's allies in episodes 2 and 5) and the **Zica** (the tunnel-world
builders who mastered thermal energy — see the Zica Laser data cube).

Every sprite is drawn the way the original ships are: 24x28 canvas, only the
game's VGA palette colours, a one-pixel dark outline, light from the upper left,
one body ramp plus one accent ramp, no dithering. Each ship has five poses like
the originals: neutral, half bank and full bank to each side (the cockpit slides
toward the turn, the lowered wing shortens and darkens).

Enable it in **Setup → Mods**. All eight ships are sold in every shop.

Prices and armor stay inside the range of the original ships at the same price
(armor never more than one point above the closest original).

Limit: the engine has 14 ship slots for all mods together. This pack (8) and
**Alien Ships** (12) cannot be enabled at the same time; Setup → Mods reports the
overflow as an error naming the ship that did not fit.
